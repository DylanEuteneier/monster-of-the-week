# Card text layouts at a glance: board games with multi-use, targeting and area-effect cards

Scope note: about 20 tool calls. Several primary sites blocked the fetcher (spiritislandwiki.com returned 403; wiki.dominionstrategy.com returned a bot check; the GMT Twilight Struggle rules URL returned 404). Where a game's layout could not be confirmed from a source, it appears under Gaps, sometimes with an unverified recollection labelled as such. The report writer should not present Gaps items as fact.

## 1. How do games show "what kind of action this is" at a glance?

### Takeaway
Confirmed patterns are (a) a fixed labelled stat bar above the effect text (Spirit Island: cost, speed, range, target), (b) colour coding of one element, such as Spirit Island's red or blue ring for Fast or Slow and Twilight Struggle's star colour for whose event it is, (c) an icon row that is the action itself (Pax Pamir court cards), and (d) named timing bands (Unmatched: Immediately / During combat / After combat). Spirit Island and Pax Pamir both keep the "where/what kind" information out of the prose and in fixed slots.

### Cited Findings
- **Spirit Island** rulebook "Anatomy of Powers" lists eight numbered regions: 1 Energy Cost, 2 Name, 3 Elements Gained, 4 Speed, 5 Range, 6 Target, 7 Effects, 8 Elemental Thresholds. — [RulesPal, Spirit Island rulebook](https://rulespal.com/spirit-island/rulebook)
- Spirit Island speed is not a word on Power Cards. It is "a red or blue ring around the Energy cost" (Fast resolves before the Invader Phase, Slow after). The kind of action is therefore read from colour, on the same spot as the cost. — [RulesPal, Spirit Island rulebook](https://rulespal.com/spirit-island/rulebook)
- **Pax Pamir 2e** court cards: core anatomy labels are Region, Suit and Rank, Card-based Action(s), and Impact Icons. Card actions are icons, and "Each card can only be used for one action once per turn, regardless of the number of actions icons on that card." — [Rally the Troops, Pax Pamir 2e rules](https://rally-the-troops.com/pax-pamir/info/rules.html)
- Pax Pamir splits two kinds of icon by when they fire. Impact icons fire on entry ("After a card is played, resolve each impact icon on the right side of the card from top to bottom"), while action icons are repeatable abilities the card grants. Impact icons place roads, spies, tribes, armies or rupees, or change the favoured suit. — [Rally the Troops, Pax Pamir 2e rules](https://rally-the-troops.com/pax-pamir/info/rules.html)
- Pax Pamir's "Advanced Anatomy" adds a Special Ability in "a small text box" and a Patriot "colored bar". Text is the exception and icons are the default. — [Rally the Troops, Pax Pamir 2e rules](https://rally-the-troops.com/pax-pamir/info/rules.html)
- The rules describe Pax Pamir's suits as covering "the vast majority of the cards ... four suits that each correspond to a different mode of power", so suit is a category of action as well as a currency. — [Rally the Troops, Pax Pamir 2e rules](https://rally-the-troops.com/pax-pamir/info/rules.html)
- **Unmatched** card text is divided into timing windows. "Immediately" resolves when combat cards are revealed. "During combat" resolves after immediate effects and before attack and defence values are compared. "After combat" resolves once combat damage has been dealt. — [the-unmatched.club glossary](https://www.the-unmatched.club/rules/glossary); rulebooks at [Restoration Games, Robin Hood v Bigfoot rules](https://restorationgames.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/07/UM-RobinHoodvBigfoot_Rules-two-page.pdf)
- Unmatched card anatomy labels: attack or defence value (if any), which fighter may use it, name, effect, BOOST value (top-left), deck, and number of copies. — [Unmatched Battle of Legends Vol. 1 instructions (Buffalo library copy)](https://www.buffalolib.org/sites/default/files/gaming-unplugged/inst/Unmatched-Battle%20of%20Legends%20Volume%20One%20Instructions.pdf), as summarised by search
- **Twilight Struggle**: the Ops value is a number in a star in the upper left. Star colour shows whose event it is (red USSR, blue US, half-and-half both or neither). An era banner sits beside the star and the card number is in the upper right. — [Misut Meeple review](https://misutmeeple.com/?p=246094); [Zatu, How to play Twilight Struggle](https://zatu.com/blogs/how-to-play/how-to-play-twilight-struggle). Both are secondary sources and differ slightly on the colour wording ("red, white, or half-and-half").
- **Gloomhaven**: a small "lost" icon in the lower right of an action marks a stronger, one-shot effect. If it is used, the card goes to the lost pile instead of the discard pile. — [Gloomhaven rulebook mirror](https://mahmati.kapsi.fi/bg/gloomhaven/gloomhaven_rulebook.html); [Order of Gamers Gloomhaven v1.1](https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/Gloomhaven_v1.1.pdf)
- **Root**: crafting cost is shown as suit icons in the card's bottom-left corner. A three-colour question mark means any suit. The suit is a fixed icon, so a card reads as currency and as recipe at the same time. — [Law of Root (unofficial copy)](https://m102.nthu.edu.tw/~s102061202/ROOT/Root_Base_Law_of_Root_web_Oct_15_2020.pdf); [Root Database, Law](https://therootdatabase.com/law/key-concepts/en)
- **Star Realms**: the faction icon is in the upper left corner and the cost (Trade symbol) in the upper right. The primary ability is the main text box. Ally abilities are flagged by a faction icon in the text box (on the left side of it). Scrap abilities are flagged by a trash-can icon in the text box. Bases designated "OUTPOST" protect your other bases. The four factions are Blob, Trade Federation, Star Empire and Machine Cult. — [RulesPal, Star Realms rulebook](https://rulespal.com/star-realms/rulebook); [RulesPal, Star Realms Academy](https://rulespal.com/star-realms-academy/rulebook)
- Star Realms timing per band: "A Ship's primary ability resolves once, when it's played." An ally ability becomes usable once another card of that faction is in play, any time in the Main Phase, at most once per turn even with several allies. Frontiers adds double-ally abilities, which need two other cards of the faction. A scrap ability removes the card permanently, and scrapping by other means does not trigger it. — [RulesPal, Star Realms Frontiers](https://rulespal.com/star-realms-frontiers/rulebook); [RulesPal Academy](https://rulespal.com/star-realms-academy/rulebook). The official FAQ says a multi-faction ability counts as both factions — [Star Realms FAQ](https://www.starrealms.com/?p=50)
- **Inis**: there are three card types (Action, Advantage, Epic Tale), and the card's colour (front and back) shows its type. Timing icons sit in the **top-left corner**: the **Season** icon means "play during the Season phase on the owner's turn" (as your action), and the **Triskel** icon means "play only at the specific timing stated on the card, usually in response to an action". A card can carry both. A **Clash** icon in the **top-right corner** marks cards that can start a clash. — [RulesPal, Inis rulebook](https://rulespal.com/inis/rulebook); [Order of Gamers Inis v2.2](https://www.orderofgamers.com/downloads/Inis_v2.2.pdf) (PDF not text-readable to the fetcher)
- Inis Triskel text opens with its trigger: Bard reads "After 1 of your maneuvers removes 1 or more opposing clans: Gain 1 Deed." The trigger clause ends with a colon, and the effect follows. — [RulesPal, Inis rulebook](https://rulespal.com/inis/rulebook)
- **The King Is Dead (2nd ed., Osprey)**: the action cards are named with one word (or "<Faction> Support"), with text explaining the effect. Each player gets the same eight: Scottish Support, Welsh Support, English Support, Negotiate, Manoeuvre, Outmanoeuvre, and two Assemble. In the advanced game the three Support cards can be replaced by three random Cunning Action cards, which have "before" or "after" in their titles. The RulesPal transcription gives names and text only, with no description of card diagrams or icons. — [RulesPal, The King Is Dead 2e rulebook](https://rulespal.com/the-king-is-dead-second-edition/rulebook)

### Inferences
- In the confirmed examples, the "kind" signal sits on a fixed spot, usually top-left or a header bar, and is shape or colour rather than a word. Spirit Island puts two signals on the same spot (cost number plus speed ring).
- Pax Pamir separates "fires once on play" from "usable repeatedly" by placing them in different zones of the card (right edge vs action row). This is a spatial cue, not a textual one.
- Unmatched shows that when one card resolves at several moments, a bold timing header in front of each paragraph keeps the prose short.

### Gaps
- No primary source fetched on Dominion's type line or card colours, because the wiki was blocked. Unverified recollection: the type line ("Action", "Action – Attack", "Treasure", "Victory") sits at the bottom with cost bottom-left. Action cards are tan or white, Treasure gold, Victory green, Reaction blue, Duration orange. The lettering is "+1 Card / +1 Action / +1 Buy / +$1" in bold, stacked as separate lines above the prose. A horizontal line divides "when played" text from text that works at other times, such as Moat's reaction or Victory card VP text.
- No source fetched on Dune: Imperium. Unverified recollection: agent icons (board-space colours) along the left edge, agent-box text in the top half, and a "Reveal" box at the bottom with persuasion and swords.
- Star Realms faction colours are not confirmed from a source. Unverified recollection: Blob green, Trade Federation blue, Star Empire yellow, Machine Cult red. Each card's frame takes its faction colour, and ability text is mostly icons (Trade coin, Combat burst, Authority, draw) with numbers, separated by horizontal bands.
- I could not confirm whether The King Is Dead cards carry a diagram of the cube movement (the coordinator expected "diagrams"). The source gave text only.
- No sources fetched for Arkham Horror LCG, Marvel Champions, Hegemony, Oath, Cascadia, Wingspan, Tapestry, John Company, Dwellings of Eldervale, Vast, Blood Rage, Kemet, Cosmic Encounter or the Slay the Spire board game. Unverified Wingspan recollection: power text sits on a colour band (brown "when activated", white "when played", pink "once between turns").

## 2. How are two or more alternative uses laid out?

### Takeaway
There are four confirmed structures. (1) A number versus a text box, where the number is a generic currency usable instead of the text (Twilight Struggle Ops star, Unmatched boost value top-left, Root's suit). (2) Two stacked text halves where exactly one is chosen (COIN unshaded top / shaded bottom; Gloomhaven top / bottom actions). (3) A row of icons where exactly one is chosen (Pax Pamir). (4) A base effect plus conditional upgrade boxes (Spirit Island thresholds). Every game sets the "choose one" rule in the rulebook, and the layout reinforces it spatially.

### Cited Findings
- **COIN series** (Fire in the Lake and others): "each card has a top event and a bottom event", which is the case for all COIN Series decks. The top (unshaded) event benefits the COIN factions (US/ARVN) and the bottom (shaded) event the insurgents (NVA/VC). "Either the top or bottom Event is allowed to be performed on a turn, never both." — [The Players' Aid, Tribesmen (FitL)](https://theplayersaid.com/2024/06/13/my-favorite-wargame-cards-a-look-at-individual-cards-from-my-favorite-games-card-4-tribesmen-from-fire-in-the-lake-insurgency-in-vietnam-from-gmt-games/); [Fire in the Lake Playbook 2018 PDF](https://nastol.io/storage/games/files/fire_in_the_lake/882365/FireLake-PLAYBOOK-2018.pdf)
- FitL "Tribesmen" in paraphrase (not verbatim card text). The unshaded side removes "any 4 Insurgent pieces total from spaces with Irregulars". The shaded side replaces Irregulars with VC Guerrillas ("tribal secession"). Volko Ruhnke ("volko26") clarified in comments that "Irregulars" means US Irregulars only. This shows how compressed event text depends on rules-defined nouns. — [The Players' Aid, Tribesmen](https://theplayersaid.com/2024/06/13/my-favorite-wargame-cards-a-look-at-individual-cards-from-my-favorite-games-card-4-tribesmen-from-fire-in-the-lake-insurgency-in-vietnam-from-gmt-games/)
- **Twilight Struggle**: a card is played for its event or for its Ops value. If it is played for Ops and carries the opponent's event, the event also triggers and the player chooses the order. Scoring cards have Ops value 0. — [Zatu, How to play Twilight Struggle](https://zatu.com/blogs/how-to-play/how-to-play-twilight-struggle); [Twilight Struggle FAQ v5](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dtompkin/archive/dtlib/base/Twilight%20Struggle%20~%20FAQ%20v5.pdf)
- Twilight Struggle asterisk: asterisked events are removed from the game after use as an event. Sources differ on the timing: one forum says they are removed on play, another says they are removed once their effect is no longer in play, and the FAQ notes exceptions such as Defectors (#103), which is not asterisked. — [Penny Arcade forum](https://forums.penny-arcade.com/discussion/176904/twilight-struggle-game-1-game-over-nuclear-holocaust-pinned-on-america); [TS FAQ v5](https://cs.uwaterloo.ca/~dtompkin/archive/dtlib/base/Twilight%20Struggle%20~%20FAQ%20v5.pdf)
- **Gloomhaven**: the regions are name, initiative number (turn order when it is the leading card), level, top action, and bottom action. Each turn a player plays two cards and uses the top of one and the bottom of the other, in either order. Any card can instead be used as a basic "Attack 2" (top) or "Move 2" (bottom) and discarded. One action can contain several abilities. Elemental augments are shown as an element symbol covered by a red augment symbol, then a colon and the augmented effect. — [Gloomhaven rulebook mirror](https://mahmati.kapsi.fi/bg/gloomhaven/gloomhaven_rulebook.html); [Dized, ability card breakdown](https://rules.dized.com/gloomhaven/rule/component-breakdown/ability-card-breakdown)
- **Unmatched**: a card can be played face down as a boost, adding its top-left number to an attack or defence or paying a BOOST effect. "Ignore any effect on the discarded card." — [the-unmatched.club glossary](https://www.the-unmatched.club/rules/glossary)
- **Root**: ambush cards (one each of mouse, rabbit and fox, plus two birds) can be spent for their suit, played before the dice roll to deal hits, or cancelled by a matching ambush. Dominance cards (one per suit) can be spent for suit but not crafted; activating one changes your victory condition and requires 10+ VP. Ordinary cards serve as suit currency, a craft (cost bottom-left), or an item or effect. — [Law of Root (unofficial copy)](https://m102.nthu.edu.tw/~s102061202/ROOT/Root_Base_Law_of_Root_web_Oct_15_2020.pdf); [UltraBoardGames Root rules](https://ultraboardgames.com/root/game-rules.php)
- **Spirit Island thresholds** are a vertical form of "alternatives": optional effects that need elements present this turn. Elements are checked, not spent. Thresholds resolve top to bottom, and a threshold marked "Instead" replaces the effects above it. — [RulesPal, Spirit Island rulebook](https://rulespal.com/spirit-island/rulebook)
- Spirit Island cards are sometimes played only for their elements, with no target, which is a second use the layout gives freely via the element icons. — [Dized, Spirit Island Q&A](https://rules.dized.com/game/_7rPptSyRn6_unBi4-2eVg/_0wT-zklR7G_EH4ijh4eIg/can-i-use-powers-if-there-is-no-eligible-target-for-them)
- **El Grande**: each action card gives a placement number (how many Caballeros you may place, shown as cubes on the card) and a special action shown as picture and text. You may skip the special action, and placement and action can be done in either order. — [BGA El Grande panel](https://en.boardgamearena.com/gamepanel?game=elgrande); [BGA Tips El Grande](https://en.doc.boardgamearena.com/Tips_elgrande)

- **Star Realms** stacks up to three abilities on one card, each in its own band of the text box with its own unlock: the primary ability (always on play), the ally ability (faction icon; unlocked by another card of that faction), and the scrap ability (trash-can icon; pay by trashing the card). Some bases print "or" between two primary abilities, and only one may be used each turn. Example: Barter World, 2 Authority **or** 2 Trade. — [RulesPal, Star Realms rulebook](https://rulespal.com/star-realms/rulebook); [RulesPal Frontiers](https://rulespal.com/star-realms-frontiers/rulebook)
- **Inis** gives an action card two possible timings rather than two effects: Season (your turn's action), Triskel (a reaction at a stated trigger), or both icons on one card. — [RulesPal, Inis rulebook](https://rulespal.com/inis/rulebook)
- **The King Is Dead** cards are single-use, single-effect, and fixed per player (all players hold the same eight). Choice comes from which card to spend and when, plus the option to pass. "When play comes back to you, you may choose to take an action as normal or pass again." Taking an action also lets you summon a follower (cube) from any region into your court, so every action card has a fixed second use as well. — [RulesPal, The King Is Dead 2e rulebook](https://rulespal.com/the-king-is-dead-second-edition/rulebook)

### Inferences
- Star Realms keys each band's unlock to an icon in the band's left margin (faction icon, trash can), so the condition is read before the effect. The same principle appears in Inis Triskel text ("After X:").
- The "generic number vs specific text" split (TS Ops, Unmatched boost, Root suit, El Grande caballero count) puts the generic value in a small corner token and gives the specific effect the large text area. The corner value needs no reading.
- The "two equal halves" split (COIN, Gloomhaven) works when both halves are the same kind of thing (two events, two actions). COIN's halves differ by faction alignment and use visual shading on the bottom half. Gloomhaven's halves differ in role (typically attack top, movement bottom, enforced by the default Attack 2 / Move 2).
- Twilight Struggle's opponent-event trigger is a case where the layout (one event box) underplays a rule interaction. The colour star carries that information.

### Gaps
- No verbatim COIN card text was obtained; Tribesmen is paraphrased. The visual treatment (unshaded top half on a light background, shaded bottom half on a grey-tinted background, faction-order symbols in a strip under the title, italic historical quote under the event title) is unverified recollection.
- Not confirmed: Twilight Struggle underlined event titles (recollection: underline means the event stays in effect, and a marker is placed).
- No sources fetched on Labyrinth or Andean Abyss specifics.

## 3. How are targets, range and geography expressed?

### Takeaway
Spirit Island puts targeting in a dedicated bar (range number or icon plus a target-land word) separate from the effect, so the effect text can assume "the target land". Gloomhaven draws area of effect as a small hex diagram in which colour marks the user and the targets. El Grande and COIN use region names or region scoring values in the prose, and Pax Pamir ties each card to a printed region.

### Cited Findings
- Spirit Island Range is "the maximum number of lands away from your Presence" and range 0 means a land with your Presence. "Terrain or placement limits, such as Sacred Sites, appear to the left of the range icon." The target bar names the land type and carries restrictions (terrain, contents, Coastal or Inland), or names a Spirit instead. Example bar: "RANGE 1. TARGET LAND: DAHAN". — [RulesPal, Spirit Island rulebook](https://rulespal.com/spirit-island/rulebook)
- Spirit Island effects "are performed in order", impossible effects are skipped, and "all effects apply to the single target land unless stated otherwise". The target bar lets effect text omit the location. — [RulesPal, Spirit Island rulebook](https://rulespal.com/spirit-island/rulebook)
- Spirit Island example stat lines: "Cost: 0. Elements: Water, Earth, Plant. Speed: Slow. Range: 0. Target: Another Spirit." and Elemental Aegis "Cost: 1. Speed: Fast. Range: 0. Target land: Any. Elements: Fire, Water, Earth." — search summary of [Dized Spirit Island Q&A](https://rules.dized.com/game/_7rPptSyRn6_unBi4-2eVg/vmrx3EgSTzyzPu-9R7KPPw/can-i-target-multiple-lands-with-a-single-power-cards). These are secondary and should be checked against card images.
- Gloomhaven area of effect is drawn as a hex diagram: "Grey indicates the hex on which the figure is currently located" (an AoE with a grey hex is always melee). "Red indicates the hexes with enemies affected by the ability." "Any rotational orientation of the depicted diagram is valid." Ranged attacks show "Range Y". For ranged AoE, "only one of the red hexes needs to be within the range specified". — [Gloomhaven rulebook mirror](https://mahmati.kapsi.fi/bg/gloomhaven/gloomhaven_rulebook.html)
- El Grande action-card geography in prose: move your own, opposing or any Caballeros, from several regions, to any region except the King's region (some cards restrict to one region). Score "specified regions". Examples: "Intrigue: Move up to 2 of your own Caballeros and 2 opposing Caballeros." "Withdrawal: Each opponent returns 3 of their Caballeros to the Province." "Outposts: Score all 4-point regions." (Dized wording, possibly paraphrased.) — [Dized, El Grande action card explanations](https://rules.dized.com/game/sJNCoKKhThyFklJ2SmGfxQ/-lEZK6JwT5KveDDOsFLb4A/action-card-explanations)
- El Grande uses region classes ("4-point regions") and a secret-disc region choice, so targeting is by category and by hidden choice rather than by adjacency. — [Dized, El Grande](https://rules.dized.com/game/sJNCoKKhThyFklJ2SmGfxQ/-lEZK6JwT5KveDDOsFLb4A/action-card-explanations)
- Pax Pamir: each court card names one of six regions, and impact icons such as tribes act "on the card's region". The card's printed region is its target. A non-ruler pays a bribe to that region's ruler to play it. — [Rally the Troops, Pax Pamir 2e rules](https://rally-the-troops.com/pax-pamir/info/rules.html)
- Root: dominance targets clearing suits (rule three clearings of the matching suit; bird dominance means two opposite corners). Card suit and board clearing suit share icons, so cards address geography by suit. — [Law of Root (unofficial copy)](https://m102.nthu.edu.tw/~s102061202/ROOT/Root_Base_Law_of_Root_web_Oct_15_2020.pdf)

- **The King Is Dead** action text, verbatim (2e), as area-control cube movement between regions:
  - Assemble: "Place a Scottish follower, a Welsh follower, and an English follower from the supply into any region or regions."
  - Scottish Support: place two Scottish followers into a region bordering a Scottish-controlled region (or bordering Moray if Moray has no control or instability disc). "If there are fewer than two Scottish followers in the supply, place as many followers as you are able to." Welsh and English Support are the same, keyed to Gwynedd and Essex.
  - Negotiate: "Swap the positions of any two face-up region cards. Place a negotiation disc on one of the cards you swapped." (This changes the order in which regions are resolved, not the cubes.)
  - Manoeuvre: "Swap a follower in any region with a follower in any other region." "If there is at least one instance where you can make the swap, you must do so."
  - Outmanoeuvre: "Swap a follower in any region with two followers in a bordering region." (If that is impossible, swap with one follower in a bordering region instead.)
  - Cunning examples: March, "Move two followers from a single region to a single bordering region." Influence, "Swap one English follower in any region with two non-English followers in another region." Edict, "Swap two Scottish followers in any region with two non-Scottish followers in a bordering region." Spy, "Resolve the effect of an action card that is face up on top of another player's discard pile." Resist, "Place two non-Scottish followers from the supply into any region that borders a region controlled by the Scots."
  - The geography vocabulary is small: "any region", "any other region", "a bordering region", "a region bordering a [faction]-controlled region". Rules-level limit: "Actions cannot place or move followers into regions that have a control or instability disc." Each region holds four followers at setup.
  — [RulesPal, The King Is Dead 2e rulebook](https://rulespal.com/the-king-is-dead-second-edition/rulebook)
- **Inis** area text uses "territory" and "adjacent territory" and "where you are present". Examples: Scouts & Spies, "move 1 or more of your clans from 1 territory to 1 adjacent territory". Craftsmen & Peasants, "In each territory where you are present, you may place 1 clan for each Citadel in that territory." — [RulesPal, Inis rulebook](https://rulespal.com/inis/rulebook)

### Inferences
- The King Is Dead gets a full action set out of three verbs (place, swap, move or return) and three region qualifiers (any, other, bordering), with faction as the noun. Its one-word names (Assemble, Manoeuvre, Outmanoeuvre, Negotiate) name the verb, not the outcome.
- There are three ways to address the map: by distance from your pieces (Spirit Island range), by a property shared between card and board (Root suit, Pax Pamir region, El Grande point value), or by a picture of the shape (Gloomhaven hexes). Shared properties need the least text.
- Pulling "where" into a header field (Spirit Island) keeps effect sentences to verb plus object ("Push 1 Explorer"-style), which suits multi-target-free single-land effects. Multi-land effects then need explicit wording ("unless stated otherwise").

### Gaps
- Exact Spirit Island card text for a multi-threshold card, and the visual form of range icons, were not retrieved (the wiki blocked the fetcher).
- El Grande's printed icons (cube count, region and scoring icons) were not confirmed from an image or the rulebook.

## 4. How is flavour set apart?

### Takeaway
There is little sourced evidence. In the confirmed sources, flavour is mostly carried by title and art rather than flavour text. Pax Pamir and Spirit Island anatomies list no flavour-text region, and the COIN historical context in the article fetched was the reviewer's background rather than card text.

### Cited Findings
- The Spirit Island rulebook's eight-region anatomy has no flavour-text region (art is not among the labelled regions either). — [RulesPal, Spirit Island rulebook](https://rulespal.com/spirit-island/rulebook)
- The Pax Pamir core and advanced anatomy labels contain no flavour text region. Cards are historical figures named on the card. — [Rally the Troops, Pax Pamir 2e rules](https://rally-the-troops.com/pax-pamir/info/rules.html)
- The Tribesmen article gives history (Montagnards of the Central Highlands, trained by US Special Forces) as commentary, not as printed text. — [The Players' Aid, Tribesmen](https://theplayersaid.com/2024/06/13/my-favorite-wargame-cards-a-look-at-individual-cards-from-my-favorite-games-card-4-tribesmen-from-fire-in-the-lake-insurgency-in-vietnam-from-gmt-games/)

### Inferences
- Games with dense targeting or alternative uses (Spirit Island, Pax Pamir, Gloomhaven) spend card area on structure and leave flavour to name and art.

### Gaps
- Unverified recollections: COIN cards print a one-line italic historical blurb directly under the event title, shaded side included. Dominion has occasional italic flavour at the bottom of the text box, absent from most cards. Arkham Horror LCG puts italic flavour text above the rules text, separated by a rule. Spirit Island has no flavour text on power cards. These need image checks.

## 5. What do designers and graphic designers say about card text, word count and readability?

### Takeaway
I found no direct statements from Reuss, Ferrin, Ruhnke or Vaccarino on templating, word count or readability within this budget. The only adjacent material is Reuss on power-card choices beyond elements, and Ruhnke's forum clarification showing reliance on defined rules terms.

### Cited Findings
- Reuss, in a Greater Than Games backer update, says spirits are most interesting when they give players reasons to care about power card drafts besides the elements. This concerns card choice design, not text. — [BackerKit, Nature Incarnate update 549](https://www.backerkit.com/c/greater-than-games/spirit-island-nature-incarnate/updates/549)
